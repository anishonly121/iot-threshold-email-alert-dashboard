WHY I BUILT ROOTWATCH

For the plants.
And the person caring for them.

My dad loves taking care of plants and gardening. But with work, he can’t check on them as often as he’d like—or always know when something has changed.

I created this project to help close that gap: sensors near the plants track their environment, the dashboard makes changes visible, and threshold alerts can send an email when readings move outside a chosen range.

— Anish Bhole, creator

01 / SENSE

Read the environment

Temperature, humidity and binary soil readings arrive through ThingSpeak.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Make changes visible

Freshness checks, threshold comparisons and historical charts add context.

03 / RESPOND

Choose the next step

Email alerts and optional AI guidance support a human care decision.
